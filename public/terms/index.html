The service

Shop Crank provides shop software for dispatch, repair orders, customers, invoices, inspections, and related back-office tools. Plans, user limits, and locations are described on the pricing page. A trial lets you create the shop before a paid plan is required for continued use.

Estimates, AI worksheets, and diagnostic suggestions are planning aids. They are not a diagnosis, a parts authorization, or a substitute for the vehicle manufacturer’s service information.

Payroll and taxes

Payroll screens calculate hours and estimated withholdings so a shop can plan a run. Those figures are not a filed tax return and are not a substitute for a payroll processor or accountant.

Diagnostics

Programming or flashing a module requires a signed repair order and a licensed interface on the shop’s own equipment. Shop Crank does not provide immobilizer bypass, key cloning, or theft-recovery tools.

Acceptable use

Availability

We work to keep the hosted app available, including an offline copy of the board on the device. Maintenance, connectivity, and third-party sign-in or payment services can interrupt access. Export tools are provided so the shop can keep a copy of its own records.
