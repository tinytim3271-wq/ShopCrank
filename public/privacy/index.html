Privacy

How shop data is handled

Last updated September 28, 2026. This page describes the Shop Crank shop application served for Your Car Guy and subscribing shops.

What the shop stores

Shop Crank keeps the records a repair shop types in: customers, vehicles, repair orders, estimates, invoices, inspections, inventory, employee profiles, time-clock entries, and messages between staff. Sign-in uses a work email and a one-time link. Shop Crank does not ask for or store shop passwords.

Who can see it

Access follows the role on the employee profile. Technicians see the jobs assigned to them. Office and admin roles see customers, invoices, and shop settings. A platform administrator can see subscribing shop accounts for support. Customer card numbers are not stored in Shop Crank; card checkout, when enabled, is completed on the payment processor’s hosted page.

Where it lives

The hosted app runs on Cloudflare. The browser also keeps a local copy so the dispatch board can open when the network blips. Signing out removes the auth session, but local records remain on the device until you clear site data. Diagnostic adapter traffic stays on the shop computer unless you explicitly save a scan to a vehicle record.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information. We do not use shop records to train a public model. AI suggestions inside the workbench are generated to help the technician on that job and should be checked against current service information.

Questions

Shop owners can correct or remove a customer record from Customers. For account deletion or a privacy request, sign in and contact the shop administrator listed on your employee profile, or write to the address on your subscription receipt.
